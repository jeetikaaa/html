Featured Project

Sahara — Emotional Support & Reflection App

Release. Reflect. Reset.
Sahara is a concept for a calm, private emotional-support application designed around helping users process difficult emotions rather than simply distracting themselves from them. 

Key Ideas

       - Mood-based interaction
       - Guided emotional release
       - Breathing and calming sessions
       - Music-based relaxation
       - Reflection after a session
       - Personalized themes and experiences
       - Future AI-based emotional-support companion

       Tech direction: Java → Android → cross-platform expansion
       The goal isn't to tell people what to feel. It's to give them a space to process it.
